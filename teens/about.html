About Us

Raising a Generation of Champions

Christ Embassy Central Church 3 Teens Church is a community committed to raising spiritually mature, purpose-driven, and excellence-minded teenagers who are passionate about God's Word and the work of the ministry.

Our Vision

Spirit-Filled Influence

To raise a generation of Spirit-filled teenagers who know Christ, live by His Word, and influence their world positively.

Our Mission

Purpose & Growth

To provide an environment where teenagers can grow spiritually, develop their gifts, build godly relationships, and discover their purpose.

Core Values

What We Stand For

Faith

Love

Welcoming every teenager with Christ's love.

Discipline

Building consistent spiritual and life habits.
