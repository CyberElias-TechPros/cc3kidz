A little help

Children's church guidelines

These simple guidelines help us keep every child safe, happy and ready to learn.

  • Arrive a few minutes early so your child can settle in before the lesson begins.
  • Sign your child in and out at the children's church desk every Sunday.
  • Tell us about any allergies, medical needs or learning support your child may need.
  • Keep your phone number and emergency contact details current with the church office.
  • Please label your child's belongings — water bottles, bags and jumpers.
  • If your child is unwell, let them rest at home and join us again next week.
  • Encourage your child to join in, make friends and ask questions.
  • Chat about the lesson on the way home — it helps faith stick!

Child protection

Keeping children safe

Because our audience includes children, safety and privacy shape everything we do — online and in person.

🔒

Privacy by design

We do not collect children's names, addresses, phone numbers or birth dates on this website. Game scores and badges are stored only on your own device.

📸

Photos with consent

Identifiable photographs or videos are published only with parental or guardian consent. We never display children's full names, schools or personal details.

🛡️

Safe spaces

There is no public messaging between children on this website. Our teachers and volunteers follow the church's child protection policy at all times.

Found something that concerns you, or have a question about child safety? Please contact the children's church office directly.
