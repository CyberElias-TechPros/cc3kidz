A special place for every child

Where little hearts meet a big God

Every Sunday, children from across Port Harcourt come together to sing, learn, play and discover how much God loves them. Here is what makes our children's church special.

📖

Learn God's Word

Discover Bible truths through stories, songs and hands-on activities — and build a strong foundation that lasts a lifetime.

🙏

Grow in Faith

Learn to pray, worship and trust God in everyday life. Children are encouraged to ask questions and grow at their own pace.

🌟

Become a Champion

Develop confidence, godly character and a sense of purpose — and discover the unique gifts God has placed inside you.
