How lessons work

Four simple steps

Every lesson follows the same gentle rhythm, so children know what to expect and parents can join in with confidence.

📖

1. Read the story

Read or listen to the Bible story together. Let children ask questions and wonder out loud.

💬

2. Talk about it

Use the questions to spark conversation. There are no wrong answers — just honest thinking.

🎨

3. Try the activity

A simple craft, game or challenge helps the lesson move from the head to the heart.

🙏

4. Pray together

Close with a short prayer. Let your child pray too — even one sentence is enough.
