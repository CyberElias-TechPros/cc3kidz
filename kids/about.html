Who we are

A place where children belong

Christ Embassy Central Church 3 Children's Church is a place dedicated to nurturing children in God's Word, helping them develop their faith, discover their gifts and grow into confident champions for Christ.

We believe children are not the church of tomorrow — they are the church of today. Every song, every story, every craft and every conversation is an opportunity to help a child understand how deeply loved they are.

Our teachers and volunteers serve with joy, patience and care. We work closely with parents because we know that faith grows best when home and church walk together.

Why we exist

Our vision & mission

Everything we do is shaped by a simple desire: to see children know God, love Him and live out His purpose.

🔭

Our Vision

To take God's divine presence to the nations and demonstrate the character of the Spirit — raising children who carry that presence into their homes, schools and city.

🌱

Our Mission

To raise a generation of Spirit-filled, Word-conscious children who demonstrate God's love, walk in faith and impact their world with excellence.
