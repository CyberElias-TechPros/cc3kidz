About Us

Christ Embassy / LoveWorld Youth

A vibrant local expression of the Christ Embassy and LoveWorld Youth Church movement in Port Harcourt, Rivers State, Nigeria.

Our Vision

Divine Presence to the Nations

To take God's divine presence to the nations of the world and demonstrate the character of the Holy Spirit, raising a generation of young people who are passionate about Jesus and committed to the advancement of God's Kingdom.

Our Mission

Spirit-Filled & Purpose-Conscious

To raise a generation of Spirit-filled, excellence-driven, and purpose-conscious young people who are equipped with the Word of God, empowered by the Holy Spirit, and committed to transforming their world.
