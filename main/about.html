About Christ Embassy

Taking God's Divine Presence to the Nations

Part of the global Christ Embassy / LoveWorld ministry, serving families, professionals, youth, and children in Port Harcourt, Rivers State.

Our Vision

Divine Presence & Character

To take God's divine presence to the nations of the world and demonstrate the character of the Holy Spirit.

Our Mission

Inheritance in Christ

To bring people into their inheritance in Christ, establish them in the Word of God, and equip them to live victorious, purpose-driven lives through the power of the Holy Spirit.

Our Values

Foundational Principles

Integrity of God's Word

Building every teaching and decision on Scripture.

Fellowship of the Spirit

Walking daily in the power and guidance of the Holy Spirit.

Soul-Winning & Evangelism

Reaching Port Harcourt and the world with the Gospel.

Love & Compassion

Caring for families, neighbours, and the community.

Excellence & Service

Giving God our very best in every ministry department.

Giving & Generosity

Partnering cheerfully in advancing God's Kingdom.
