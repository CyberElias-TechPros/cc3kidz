A class for every age

Every child learns differently. Our classes are designed around age, stage and a whole lot of care — so each child can meet God in a way that makes sense to them.

🎒
🕐

When we meet

Sundays, during the main service. Children join their classes after a time of family worship.

📍

Where we meet

Dedicated children's classrooms at Christ Embassy Central Church 3 Group, PH Zone 3.

🎒

What to bring

A Bible if you have one, a water bottle and a smile. We provide everything else for the lesson.

💙

Who is welcome

Every child aged 0–12. Classes are a guide — we will help you find the best fit for your child.

Ages 0–2
Creche / Toddlers

Gentle beginnings

Our creche is a soft, safe and happy space where our littlest ones are cared for, sung to and loved.

While you enjoy the service, your baby or toddler is welcomed into a calm room with age-appropriate toys, gentle music and caring helpers. We sing simple songs about Jesus, read sturdy board-book Bible stories and pray blessing over each child.

Primary

Discovering truth

Children learn Bible basics, memorise verses and discover how to live out their faith every day.

These are the years when faith begins to become personal. Through stories, games, quizzes and small-group conversations, children learn who God is, how to pray and what it means to follow Jesus at home and at school.

A typical Sunday

What to expect

From the moment you arrive to the moment you pick up your child, we want Sunday to be easy and joyful for your whole family.

1️⃣

Check-in

Arrive a few minutes early and check your child in at the children's church desk. A team member will guide you to the right class.

2️⃣

Worship

Children begin in the main service for a time of family worship, then head to their classes with their teachers.

3️⃣

Class time

A Bible story, songs, games, crafts and small-group time — all tailored to your child's age group.

4️⃣

Pick-up

Collect your child from their classroom after the service. Teachers will share a little about what they learned.

Class ages are a guide. If you are unsure which class is right for your child, speak to a team member on Sunday or send us an enquiry — we will gladly help.
